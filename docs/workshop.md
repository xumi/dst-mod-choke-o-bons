## Title

Choke-o-Bons

## Short description (one line, for the item card)

A rare bag of chocolate bonbons with a secret number of treats. Eat it and feel better... until the bag runs dry.

## Description

**A rare treat. Handle with care.**

Choke-o-Bons are bags of chocolate bonbons scattered sparingly across the world. Each bag holds a *secret* number of treats, from 1 to 8, and you never get to see how many are left.

**Eating one**
- Restores a little sanity (+20) and hunger (+1)
- Costs you a sliver of health (-1)
- Makes you cheer, and the bag stays in your inventory

**Eating an empty bag**
- Costs you 100 sanity
- Your character breaks down in tears and says something fittingly hollow (every character has their own line)
- The bag falls apart

You never know which bite is the last one. Be careful with that last point of health. The health cost can kill you.

**Where to find them**
- A handful are scattered across the surface world when it is first created (3 by default)
- Pigs, merms and bunnymen very occasionally drop one when killed (0.5% by default)
- The Pig King very occasionally tosses one out along with his gold when you trade him a trinket (1% by default)

**Settings** (all adjustable when you create the world)
- Items on the map: 3, 6 or 12
- Mob drop chance: 0.1%, 0.5% or 1%
- Pig King chance: 1%, 3% or 5%

**Notes**
- This mod must be enabled by everyone on the server.
- The map items are placed when the world first loads, so enabling the mod on an existing world scatters them once.
- Works with every vanilla character; modded characters get a default line.

Found a bug or have an idea? Leave a comment or open an issue on GitHub: https://github.com/xumi/dst-mod-choke-o-bons
